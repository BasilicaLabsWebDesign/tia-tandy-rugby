In Her Own Words

Thurrock T-Birds v Richmond

Sunday 11 October 2026 · Women’s Championship · Home

Thurrock T-Birds 0–56 Richmond Ladies

Result — Loss Full 80 minutes 18 tackles, none missed Held up a try Self-rated 7/10

From the day — Thurrock T-Birds v Richmond at Oakfield

Match Overview

It was another tough afternoon for Thurrock T-Birds as we suffered a 56–0 defeat against Richmond Ladies. We went into the game with just 16 players available to face a squad of 22, making an already challenging fixture even more demanding.

Richmond are an exceptionally strong side, having won the league championship for the last three seasons. Despite being unable to secure promotion because of facility requirements, they have continued to demonstrate the quality and experience of a top-level team. They also benefit from having paid players, whereas we are an amateur club managing four teams and often struggling to field a full side.

We knew we were facing a difficult challenge, but conceding from the opening play was a disappointing start. We quickly found ourselves 17–0 down, and the game threatened to get away from us early.

However, we regrouped, dug deep and continued to battle. Despite spending long periods under pressure, we created opportunities of our own and came close to getting on the scoreboard. Unfortunately, Richmond’s defensive strength and our own individual errors made it difficult to turn those opportunities into points.

Although the final score was 56–0, I don’t feel it fully reflects the fight we showed or the opportunities we created. Richmond were the better side on the day, but there were periods where we competed well despite the difficult circumstances.

Individual Performance Review

Personally, I felt I put in a solid shift at hooker and rated my performance 7/10.

My biggest positive was my defensive contribution. I recorded 18 tackles without missing any, maintaining a high level of involvement throughout the full 80 minutes. I also managed to hold up a Richmond try, preventing them from adding to their score on that occasion.

Playing the full match with a limited squad required considerable physical and mental effort. Despite the pressure we faced, I continued to work hard defensively and looked for opportunities to contribute.

There are still areas I want to improve, particularly in helping the team build pressure and make the most of our possession. However, I can take confidence from my defensive performance and the fact that I maintained my involvement throughout the game.

Personal Highlights

  • 18 tackles made, with no missed tackles.
  • Held up an opposition try.
  • Played the full 80 minutes.
  • Maintained my defensive work rate despite sustained pressure.
  • Rated my overall performance 7/10.

Team Performance: Positives and Areas to Improve

Positives

  • We regrouped after a difficult opening and continued to compete.
  • We created opportunities and came close to scoring.
  • Despite having only 16 players available, the team continued to work hard.
  • There were periods where we challenged Richmond and showed determination under pressure.

Areas to Improve

  • Starting stronger: Conceding on the opening play put us immediately on the back foot. We need to be more organised and focused from kick-off.
  • Reducing individual errors: A number of Richmond’s tries came from mistakes that gave them opportunities to exploit. Improving our decision-making and execution will help us stay in games for longer.
  • Managing pressure: Against strong opposition, maintaining possession, improving our discipline and making better decisions under pressure are essential.
  • Squad availability: Having only 16 players against a squad of 22 placed us at a significant disadvantage. Getting more players back will improve our depth, energy and ability to compete across the full match.

Looking Ahead

This was a difficult result, but we need to keep it in perspective. Richmond are a very strong and experienced side, and we faced them with a depleted squad.

The 56–0 scoreline hurts, but it doesn’t mean there were no positives to take away. We created opportunities, continued to fight and showed resilience in difficult circumstances.

From a personal perspective, recording 18 tackles with no misses and holding up a try gives me something positive to build on. My focus is to maintain that defensive standard while continuing to improve my overall contribution as a hooker.

As a team, the priority is to get more players back, build consistency in training and cut out the individual errors that are costing us. We know we have work to do, but with a stronger squad and better execution, there is plenty of opportunity to turn our season around.

Final Reflection

A tough afternoon against a very good Richmond side, made harder by our limited numbers. The result was disappointing, but I can take confidence from my individual defensive performance. We need to learn from the mistakes, stick together and use this experience as motivation to improve in the coming weeks.
