In Her Own Words

Thurrock T-Birds v Old Albanian Saints

Sunday 18 October 2026 · Women’s Championship · Away

Thurrock T-Birds 0–37 Old Albanian Saints

Result — Loss Full 80 minutes Held up a try Blocked a try Self-rated 7/10

From the day — Thurrock T-Birds v Old Albanian Saints at Woollams

A Better Performance Despite Another Tough Result

Our third league game brought another difficult challenge, this time away against Old Albanian Saints, who won 37–0. However, despite the scoreline, I felt this was a much better performance from us and a real step forward as a team.

For the first time this season, we went into a game with substitutes available, with 20 players against their 22. It might not sound like a huge difference, but after struggling so badly for numbers in our opening games, having some cover on the bench was a positive sign that things are starting to improve.

Our first three fixtures have been against Bath, Richmond and Old Albanian Saints — three of the strongest sides in the league. Old Albanians had also beaten Beckenham 83–5 in their previous game, the biggest defeat Beckenham had suffered against any opponent so far. We knew we were facing another major test.

The Match

For the first time this season, I really felt we started to play as a team. We worked hard for one another, competed throughout and created several opportunities to get on the scoreboard. Unfortunately, Old Albanians had a very strong defence, and every time we looked capable of making something happen, we struggled to find that final bit of execution.

At half-time, we were 17–0 down. That was frustrating because we conceded a try and conversion in the dying minutes of the half. Going into the break at 10–0 would have felt very different, particularly after the effort we had put in to stay competitive.

We came out positively in the second half and again came close to scoring. However, a few mistakes continued to hurt us. We would work hard to get into good positions, only to lose possession or make an error that allowed the opposition to relieve the pressure and push us back around 40 metres. We then had to work our way up the pitch all over again.

We managed to hold them out for the first 15 minutes of the second half before they scored again. As the game went on, a few more tries followed, particularly towards the end, and we eventually lost 37–0.

The result was disappointing, but I genuinely don’t think it reflected the progress we made or how competitive parts of the game were. We created opportunities, defended strongly for periods and, most importantly, started to look more like a team.

We still have plenty to work on, but there were encouraging signs.

Individual Performance Review

Personally, I felt I had another solid game at hooker and rated my performance 7/10.

Defensively, I made 16 tackles and missed one. I was heavily involved throughout the match, recording 26 rucks and carrying the ball 11 times for a total gain of 54 metres.

I also made five completed passes, received 11 passes and contributed to several important moments around the pitch.

My highlights included diving on two loose balls to retain possession, holding up a try, blocking a try, contributing to a long driving maul, making a steal and catching a lineout ball before making ground.

These are the sorts of contributions I want to keep building on. As a hooker, I want to be involved in the physical side of the game while also helping the team retain possession, create opportunities and put pressure on the opposition.

There were still areas I could improve, particularly in reducing mistakes and making sure we turn promising positions into points. However, I felt my overall contribution was positive.

Looking Ahead

The biggest positive is that our numbers are beginning to improve. Having 18 players available rather than struggling to field a side gives us something to build on, and hopefully we can continue getting more players back into the squad.

Our opening three fixtures have been extremely challenging, facing Bath, Richmond and Old Albanian Saints in succession. These are the sorts of games where mistakes get punished, and we have certainly learned some hard lessons.

Now, with those opening fixtures behind us, I hope we can start building some momentum, cut out the errors that keep putting us under pressure and turn our improved performances into results.

We know we need to improve, but I believe there is more to come from this group. If we can keep developing as a team, improve our consistency and get more players available, there is no reason we cannot start putting some wins on the board.

Final Reflection

Another tough result, but a much better team performance. We competed, created opportunities and showed signs of coming together after a difficult start to the season. Personally, I was pleased with my contribution, particularly my defensive work and involvement around the pitch.

The scoreline says 37–0, but there were positives to take from this game. Now we need to learn from the mistakes, keep working hard and turn that progress into results.

16 tackles, 54 metres gained, 26 rucks and another 80 minutes completed. We keep going. 💪🏉
