Match Reviews

Every game reviewed by Tia herself — how it unfolded, what went well, and what gets worked on next. Newest first.

Sunday 27 September 2026 · Women’s Championship · Away

Thurrock T-Birds 12–31 Bath

Result — Loss Try scorer

Tia’s try at Bath — driving over from ten metres out

We knew we were in for a tough afternoon at Bath. We had a number of players unavailable, including our captain, while some of the players stepping in hadn’t even trained with us before. We arrived with just 16 players, meaning only one substitute, while Bath had a full squad of 22 and looked very organised during the warm-up.

The first half started badly for us. After a few missed tackles, we quickly found ourselves 5–0 down. Within the first 10 minutes, that became 12–0 after Bath scored from a driving maul off a lineout, and suddenly we had a real uphill battle on our hands.

Another driving maul from a lineout brought Bath’s third try. The conversion was missed, but we were now 17–0 down.

To our credit, we fought our way back into the game. We put Bath under pressure inside their own 22, forced them to defend, and eventually worked the ball wide to score a really good try. A great conversion brought the score back to 17–7 and gave us some much-needed belief.

Unfortunately, just before half-time, a dropped pass was picked up by Bath and they were able to run in a soft try, which they converted. We went into the break 24–7 down.

With Bath able to bring on fresh legs at half-time and our numbers already limited, things were looking even tougher for the second half. We were also starting to pick up a few knocks.

After losing a scrum and some poor defensive work, Bath were able to break through and score another converted try, taking the score to 31–7.

At times we were down to just 13–14 players on the pitch, with players battling through injuries, but the ladies showed an incredible amount of heart and grit. We refused to give up and managed to pull another try back.

That one was mine, from around 10 metres out. I managed to battle through three players to get the ball down. Unfortunately, the conversion came back off the post.

We actually finished the game strongly and were unlucky not to score again, which says a lot about the character of the team given everything we had to deal with.

Yes, we lost, but I have to say I’m incredibly proud of the girls. With new players coming into the side, only 16 players available, no real opportunity to train together as a full squad, and the way the first half went, it would have been easy to fold and let the game turn into a big defeat.

Instead, we battled right to the final whistle.

Plenty to work on, but also plenty to be proud of. 💪🏉

Sunday 6 September 2026 · Pre-season friendly · Away

Thurrock T-Birds 22–40 Shelford RFC

Result — Loss Position — Hooker Full game Forward of the Game

From the day — Thurrock T-Birds at Shelford

Going into the game, we were already up against it. We only had 10 players available due to people being unable to make it and a few last-minute cancellations. It was therefore agreed that we would play 13-a-side, with Shelford kindly loaning us three players. The game was also changed to four 20-minute quarters.

Despite the circumstances, I was really surprised by how well we started. The opening quarter was a very even contest, with both teams working hard and creating opportunities. The score remained 0–0 until around the 15th minute, when we conceded a try after attempting to kick our way out of danger in our own red zone. The ball fell to one of their players, who managed to run in and score. They were unable to convert, making it 0–5.

We responded almost immediately, scoring and converting our own try to take a 7–5 lead.

The second quarter was another cagey affair, with both teams having their chances. Shelford eventually managed to break through and scored from a penalty. I was pretty confident that I had held the player up over the line, but the referee saw it differently and awarded the try. With the conversion, Shelford regained the lead at 7–12.

Once again, we refused to let our heads drop and managed to respond before half-time, bringing the score level at 12–12. We also lost a player to injury, so would head into the second half 12 against 13.

It was a tough game, made even more challenging by the hot and humid conditions. With such a limited number of players, fatigue inevitably started to set in. Shelford were able to make full use of their much larger 27-player squad, while we really struggled during the third quarter. They took advantage and pulled away, leaving us 12–33 behind.

Despite the difficult third quarter, we found another gear in the fourth. We scored early and then added another try, showing that we weren’t prepared to give up. Shelford responded with scores of their own, and the final whistle went with the score at 22–40.

On a personal level, I was really pleased to be awarded Forward of the Game. It’s always nice to get some recognition for the effort put in, especially in such challenging circumstances.

There are definitely areas we need to improve, but there are also plenty of positives to take from the game. We were massively short on numbers, playing in difficult weather conditions against a team with a much bigger squad, but we never quit.

We fought right until the final whistle, and that’s something we can take forward into what will be a tough season ahead.

The only way is up from here. 🏉
